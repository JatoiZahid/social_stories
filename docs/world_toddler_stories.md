# World story additions

Six original, short English retellings for a grown-up to read aloud with a
toddler. These are selections from established children's storytelling
traditions, not a ranked worldwide popularity list. Each has six static
illustrations, two short sentences per page, and two optional two-choice
questions for shared discussion. Traditional tales travel across borders;
the associations below describe the traditions consulted, not exclusive origins.

| Story | Tradition / context | Selection evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Enormous Turnip | Russian cumulative folktale | [Syllabes children's book listing](https://www.syllabes-agency.com/the-enormous-turnip/) identifies the tradition and an edition for age 2+. |
| The Little Red Hen | English-language traditional farm tale | [Ladybird First Favourite Tales catalogue](https://wp.penguin.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Ladybird-Web-Stocklist-Catalogue-2024.pdf) includes it in its traditional story collection. |
| The Cap Seller and the Monkeys | South Asian tale, including Indian and Nepali retellings | [Katha Kids](https://kathakids.com/folktales/the-monkeys-and-the-capseller/) and [Global Story Connection](https://www.globalstoryconnection.org/southeast-asia/thehatseller) show regional retellings. |
| The Great Race | Chinese zodiac tale | [British Council LearnEnglish Kids](https://learnenglishkids.britishcouncil.org/listen-watch/short-stories/great-race) presents the animal race and zodiac context. |
| Anansi and the Pot of Wisdom | Ghanaian / Akan storytelling tradition | [Smartline Publishers](https://smartlinepublishers.com/product/ananse-and-the-pot-of-wisdom/?v=93d23d0fde0b) lists the tale in its Ghanaian folklore series. |
| The Ugly Duckling | Hans Christian Andersen, Denmark | [Andersen's World](https://andersenworld.com/hans-christian-andersen) identifies the Danish author's story; [British Council](https://learnenglishkids.britishcouncil.org/category/topics/traditional-stories) includes a child-oriented retelling. |

The text is newly written for this app and does not reproduce modern editions.
The Little Red Hen ends with the friends helping next time. The Ugly Duckling
keeps the swan discovery while replacing prolonged mistreatment with reassurance;
kindness does not depend on appearance. Anansi shares wisdom voluntarily rather
than throwing a pot. The race is shortened without describing every finish place.
These are deliberate gentle adaptations, not full traditional versions.

Artwork uses the built-in imagegen tool, one story sheet per tale, split into
page assets under `assets/images/story_scenes/<story_id>_scene_01.png` through
`_06.png`. Prompts are recorded in `world_toddler_artwork.json`.
