## Runtime layers
`GaragePainter` draws the separate body image, eyes/blinks, mouth expressions, headlights, wheels, toolbox, bonnet, bolt, rotating wrench, sparkles, road lane markings and finish marker. Road artwork pans with distance. Transparent source alpha is preserved. PNGs are not substituted as whole-screen animation frames. One decoded copy of each dynamic illustration is retained and disposed on exit.
