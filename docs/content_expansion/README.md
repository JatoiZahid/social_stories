# Stories and paired puzzle sets

Five new, original gentle retellings follow the existing story format: six
illustrated pages, two short text fields per page, existing text-to-speech
narration, and two end-of-story multiple-choice questions. The additions are
Goldilocks and the Three Bears, The Gingerbread Man, The North Wind and the Sun,
The Fox and the Stork, and The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse. There are now
31 stories. The existing 12 animated social stories retain their action atlases;
the 19 classic tales use static scene illustrations.

The retellings soften frightening outcomes: Goldilocks apologizes and leaves
with a grown-up; the gingerbread man stops at the stream and returns safely;
Fox and Stork learn to choose dishes that suit each friend. They are adaptations
rather than reproductions of full traditional versions or modern book text.

Five new puzzle pictures show Ant & Grasshopper together in five distinct scenes.
The 20 earlier pictures remain available (25 playable puzzles total).
Ant & Grasshopper artwork uses 4:3 framing; existing wider pictures retain their
original proportions in the board, guide, draggable feedback and pieces.

Cars (Lightning McQueen & Mater), Mario & Luigi, and Mickey & Minnie are queued
at the user's explicit request. The image service rejected those requests.
Their fifteen planned entries are retained in `queued_puzzles.json` and
`queued_puzzles.dart.txt`; no missing-art entries are exposed in the app.

All art was generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The new story pictures
are under `assets/images/story_scenes/`; puzzle pictures are under
`assets/images/puzzles/`. Exact prompts are recorded in `artwork_prompts.json`.
Review sheets are retained alongside this note. The original launcher icon,
parent gate, and saved blurred-guide/plain preference remain unchanged.
