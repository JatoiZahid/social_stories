The eight classic fables use six static illustrations each. The existing social
stories retain their action animations. The first three fables are bundled in
`assets/images/story_scenes/` under the `ant_and_grasshopper`, `ant_and_pigeon`,
and `clever_crow` prefixes. Each sheet was generated with the built-in imagegen
tool, inspected, and split into six page images in reading order.

See `fable_artwork_expansion.md` for the five additional fables and their prompts.
